What is “MINDWELL”?

Body, Mind, and Energy are the three indivisible and equally important aspects of human nature. The key to a healthy and prosperous life lies in the capability to manage those aspects well.

The power of the Mind to consciously choose thoughts, emotions and reactions for the purpose of our well-being lies at the core of the “mind well” concept. This power is our innate ability, which however needs to be unleashed.

Often failure to “mind well” lies in blocked emotions and destructive mental patterns which are firmly sealed in our body. Energy work helps to release imbalances and traumas and let your body feel the new state, which your Mind learns to consciously choose.

MINDWELL is a place where energy gently heals your body, restores your inner harmony and lovingly guides you to the new state of mind so you can live a healthier and happier life.

About

Certified Reiki master, energy and crystal healer.

Welcome, beautiful 🤍

My name is Anna Sol. I’m a certified Reiki Master, energy and crystal healer. I’m also a wife and a mother of 2 beautiful children.

I came to Reiki in 2020 while being in a deep identity crisis and searching for help.

My Reiki education was long and exhaustive. At the beginning I was very skeptical about energy work overall. I could not believe that something you can’t see and, in most cases, feel can change your life. I really wanted to challenge myself, dive into my own distrust, and see if Reiki can help me.

As a result I spent 3.5 years on my education.

I took a couple of long breaks after finishing my first and second Reiki degrees just to make sure that I had enough space to go through deep internal transformations and very hard self-work while constantly practicing. This allowed me to not only heal my traumas and mental programs, but also to gain significant experience and understanding of energy healing mechanisms. I also felt that my path was to share Reiki with people so they can live healthier and happier lives.

How I work

I offer clients distant Reiki sessions followed by the summary and information coming in the flow, which contains important “keys” for the patient’s healing at this moment of time. Knowing the root causes of imbalance is crucial for healing.

For this reason I also offer deeper sessions, which include diagnostics of aura and body through chakras and the method of non-traditional art therapy. Diagnostics makes it possible to dive into the root causes of the client’s state, be it a childhood trauma, an ancestors’ program or a past-life experience, and heal it. I also accompany Reiki sessions by crystals and natural stones, whose healing abilities intensify under the flow of universal energy.

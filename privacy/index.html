Privacy

What this website does, and does not do, with your information.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who runs this website

MINDWELL is run by Anna Sol. For any question about your information, write to mindwellreiki@gmail.com.

What the website collects

While you browse, the website itself collects nothing about you. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or fonts and scripts loaded from other companies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address and browser type when a page is loaded, and handles it under its own privacy statement.

The contact form

If you send an inquiry, I receive the name, email address, optional phone number and message you type. I use them only to reply to you and to arrange the consultation or sessions you ask for. I do not sell them or use them for advertising.

When you press “Send inquiry”, the form opens your own email app with the message ready to send, so your inquiry travels by email directly from you to me. This website does not receive or store it.

Your message may touch on your health or wellbeing. Share only what you are comfortable sharing; you can always say more later.

I keep correspondence for as long as it is needed to help you and to follow up on what you ask for, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you by writing to the address above. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Links to other sites

Links to Instagram, X, Substack and WhatsApp take you away from this website. Those services have their own privacy practices.

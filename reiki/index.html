Reiki

“The secret art of inviting happiness, the miraculous medicine for all diseases”

Mikao Usui

Reiki is a natural healing modality that uses universal life force energy to improve well-being.

The word “Reiki” comes from Japanese:

Rei
“universal”
Ki
“life energy”

Reiki is the highly vibrational energy of creation, the ultimate essence of the Universe. It’s the energy of pure unconditional love and a “breath of life” that flows through all living beings.

Where Reiki comes from

The healing power of Reiki was known to humanity since ancient times. However, access to this knowledge was strictly limited. Reiki was re-opened to the world by Dr. Mikao Usui in Japan in the early 1900s. He established the modern form of this energetic practice and formulated 5 fundamental principles for healing and overall well-being.

Hands-on, or from a distance

A Reiki practitioner acts as a guide between the recipient and the source of the universal life force energy. The energy flows to the patient through the Master’s hands. For this reason Reiki is often called “palm” or “hands-on” energy healing.

Reiki energy healing can be performed remotely, without the need for physical contact. Life-force energy connects all living beings and is not limited by space and time, so it can be sent and received over any distance. Distant Reiki has the same effectiveness as an in-person session. This makes remote energy sessions ideal for people with a busy schedule and allows you to enjoy all the benefits of Reiki healing in the comfort of your home.

Not a religion, and not dependent on belief

Reiki is not a religion and it is not dependent on belief. It’s a simple, natural and safe method of energy healing that everyone can use. It doesn’t have any contraindications and always creates a beneficial effect. It is being used all over the world, including hospitals and hospices, to complement other forms of health treatment and promote recovery.

Reiki brings balance to our bodies on all levels: physical, emotional, mental and spiritual

It promotes self-healing abilities, self-empowerment, spiritual development, and enhances quality of life

5 principles for a harmonious and happy life

Mikao Usui, the creator of the Reiki spiritual practice, also introduced 5 principles that he believed are the key to living a happy life and serve as “medicine to the soul”. These principles help to incorporate Reiki energy into your daily life and encourage the self-healing process.

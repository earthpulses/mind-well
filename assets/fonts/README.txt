Self-hosted fonts (no requests to Google or any third party)

  Cormorant Garamond (variable, weights 300-700, upright + italic)  - headings and quotes
  Jost (variable, weights 100-900)                                  - body text and UI

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits self-hosting and redistribution.
Full licence texts are in this folder. Files are the Latin and Latin-Extended subsets (covers English
and Central/Eastern European names such as Kouřilová) distributed by the Fontsource project
(https://fontsource.org), built from the Google Fonts releases.
